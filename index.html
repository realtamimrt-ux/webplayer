Basic controls
Big play button, seek bar with buffering, time, volume, and full screen. Works with mouse, touch, and keyboard.

    
Advanced controls
Speed 0.25×–2×, ±10s skip, chapters with markers, loop, fill/fit, picture-in-picture, and keyboard shortcuts (press ?).

    
Dashboard tutorial widget
Floating corner player you can drag, minimize, enlarge, and close. Once closed it stays closed and leaves a small "Watch" button.

    
Remembers viewers
Resumes where each viewer stopped, and keeps their volume. Nothing to set up.

    
Mobile friendly
Double-tap left or right to skip 10 seconds, tap to show controls, and the layout adapts to small widths.

    
Never a dead player
If Google won't stream a file directly, the player automatically switches to Google Drive's own player.

  


  

    

      
Make your Drive video public

      
    
        
  1. Open the video in Google Drive and click Share.
    2. 
        
  2. Under General access, choose Anyone with the link (Viewer).
    3. 
        
  3. Click Copy link and paste it above.
    4. 
      

      
For the most reliable custom player on big files, add a free Google API key with the Drive API enabled: api-key="…". Without one, DrivePlayer streams directly when Google allows it and falls back to the Drive player when it doesn't.

    

    

      
Open from any button

      
Any element with data-drive-src opens the video in a lightbox. No extra code.

      
<button data-drive-src="DRIVE_LINK"
        data-title="How to export">
  ▶ Watch how
</button>
