Five minutes to a cleaner kitchen workflow

By the Team · 6 min read

We filmed the whole process so you can follow along. The video below lives in a public Google Drive folder — no YouTube channel, no ads, no recommendations pulling readers away.

Start with the counters. Everything that doesn't belong in the kitchen goes into one basket, which you'll sort later. This step alone usually takes under a minute.

Short on time?

Then work top to bottom: shelves, counters, stovetop, then floor. Gravity is on your side — crumbs fall down, so you never clean the same spot twice.
